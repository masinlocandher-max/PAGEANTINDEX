# PageantIndex Launch Blueprint

## Vision

PageantIndex is building the global professional identity and relationship infrastructure for pageantry, starting with public profiles, verified professional history, and a connected industry network.

PageantIndex is global from day one. The Philippines is the first market actively populated and operationalized. Future country operators are commercial expansion partners, not owners of separate PageantIndex products.

## Launch-facing product

Only three visible product surfaces are part of launch:

1. Landing Page
2. Dashboard / Profile Creation
3. Public Profile

The launch experience serves three primary identity types:

- Supplier / Professional
- Candidate / Titleholder
- Pageant Organization

Basic profiles remain free because completeness of the index is itself a strategic asset.

## Relationship graph

Core relationship model:

Pageant Name + Year / Edition + Candidate + Supplier + Role

Candidate is optional for organization-level official supplier relationships and required for candidate-specific supplier relationships.

Examples:

- Photographer for Candidate X · Miss Pageant 2026
- Official Photographer · Miss Pageant 2026

These are separate relationships. A candidate-specific professional must not be presented as an official pageant supplier unless the organization confirms that role.

## Trust layers

Identity Verified
: PageantIndex confirms the person using identity evidence and selfie/liveness.

Candidate Confirmed
: A candidate confirms that a supplier worked directly with her in the stated role.

Organization Confirmed
: A pageant organization confirms an official pageant-level role.

Self-added credits remain unconfirmed until the relevant candidate or organization approves them.

## Organization workflow

Organizations can:

- claim their profiles
- add authorized admins
- manage pageant editions
- add candidates
- invite official suppliers through PageantIndex links
- confirm official pageant-level roles

## Candidate workflow

Candidates can:

- create or claim profiles
- maintain pageant history
- invite personal suppliers
- confirm supplier-submitted candidate-specific credits

## Supplier workflow

Suppliers can:

- create or claim free professional profiles
- manage identity, category, location, bio and services
- publish portfolios
- add historical pageant credits
- receive invitations
- request confirmation of self-added credits
- manage verification and confirmation indicators

## Public profile

The public profile is a polished, shareable professional page available without login. It can show:

- identity
- category
- location
- bio
- services
- portfolio
- pageant credits
- candidates and organizations worked with
- confirmation indicators
- verification status
- official links
- contact options

## SEO and AI discovery

Public profiles should use structured, machine-readable information that clearly identifies who someone is, what they do, where they work, and their pageant relationships. PageantIndex does not promise Google rankings or AI recommendations.

## Growth loop

Partners → organizations → candidates → suppliers → historical credits → more candidates and organizations → more users → more partner value.

Benefits-first growth means securing useful hotel and airline privileges, then inviting pageant organizations to claim free profiles and bring candidates, teams and suppliers into the network.

PageantIndex Privileges remains separate from pageant credits and relationship confirmation.

## Later ecosystem

Hotels, airlines, tourism, ticketing, voting, tabulation, analytics, advertising, booking, and business tools remain part of the broader architecture. They do not appear as launch-facing modules until ready.

## Long-term moat

The moat is the relationship graph: who worked with whom, in what pageant, in what year, in what role, and who confirmed it.
