Trust desk

Report a concern or exercise a privacy right.

Use this form for impersonation, copyright, harassment, fraud, privacy, privacy-rights requests, safety, or other integrity concerns. Do not include passwords, full government ID numbers, payment-card data, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Submit a report or request

What happens next

Accepted reports and requests receive a PageantIndex case reference. The trust desk triages the submission, may request reasonable information to verify identity or authority, and records the resolution internally.

Privacy rights

Choose Privacy rights request for access, correction, deletion or blocking, portability, objection, restriction, or another data-subject request. PageantIndex may need to verify that the request concerns your own information before acting.

Urgent safety

If someone is in immediate physical danger, contact the appropriate local emergency authority. This PageantIndex form is an operational reporting channel, not an emergency service.

Copyright

Identify the work, where it appears on PageantIndex, your relationship to the work, and a source that helps establish ownership or authorization.

Impersonation

Identify the profile or account, the person or organization being impersonated, and evidence that helps establish the authentic identity or authorized representative.

Read the Member Privacy Policy or PageantIndex trust and verification standards.
